## Every feature ends with a visual explainer (mandatory)

Rule from Jay (2026-10-27), for every project: anything you ship or stage that a user will notice finishes with **one illustrated English explainer page**. Without it the feature is not done.

- **Contents, in order:** a status strip (each change tagged `live now` or `waiting on you`); per feature a 16:9 sketch, a caption, numbered how-to-use steps naming real buttons, and "what was wrong" for fixes; how it got built (failures stated plainly); what is waiting on Jay (copyable commands, batched questions); code locations.
- **Sketches:** Ian Xiaohei style, in **English only**. Pure white background, thin wobbly black ink, lots of empty space. Xiaohei (a solid black blob with white dot eyes and thin legs, deadpan) performs the core action. 3–6 short notes: red = problem, orange = flow, blue = system. No slides, flowcharts, screenshots or cute art. Credit Ian Xiaohei Illustrations (MIT) in the footer.
- **Publish** it (an artifact, or the project's docs), and link it in the session log and the final reply.
